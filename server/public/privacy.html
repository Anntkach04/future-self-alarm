Privacy Policy

Future Self Alarm · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Future Self Alarm (“the App”) helps you wake up with a message spoken in a voice clone of your own voice. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is designed to meet Apple App Store expectations for a clear, accessible privacy policy linked from the App and App Store metadata.

Who we are

The App is operated by the Future Self Alarm team (Dyniza). For privacy questions, data access, or deletion requests, use our Contact Us form (topic: Privacy / data request). That form is saved on our servers so messages are not lost in email spam filters.

What we collect

  • Account. If you Sign in with Apple, we store your Apple user identifier (sub) and, if Apple provides it, your email. We do not receive your Apple password.
  • Voice sample. Audio you record to create a voice clone is sent to our servers and to our voice provider (ElevenLabs) to create and use your clone. We store the resulting voice ID so we can generate morning alarms. Raw samples are processed to deliver the feature and are not sold.
  • Profile answers. Name, goals, tone preferences, music bed choice, morning habits, and similar onboarding answers used to personalize scripts.
  • Mood check-ins. Optional mood and note that can shape the next morning’s wake message.
  • Subscription status. Handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We receive entitlement / purchase status needed to unlock membership features. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Device / diagnostics. Basic technical logs needed to run and debug the service (for example API errors). We do not sell personal data and we do not use your voice or profile for advertising.

How we use data

  • To clone your voice and generate alarm audio
  • To personalize morning scripts and mood-based lines
  • To restore your profile after you sign in again
  • To provide and manage membership / free trial features
  • To respond when you contact us

Processors (third parties)

We use trusted service providers only as needed to run the App. They process data on our instructions:

  • ElevenLabs — voice cloning and text-to-speech
  • OpenAI — short mood encouragement text, when enabled
  • Our hosting provider — API, database, and file storage
  • RevenueCat and Apple — subscriptions and receipts

Permissions on your device

  • Microphone — to record your voice sample (requested when you start recording).
  • Speech recognition — to guide onboarding karaoke highlighting while you read (requested when you start recording).
  • Notifications / AlarmKit — to wake you with your message (requested on the Allow alarms step).

You can decline any permission; related features will be limited. You can change permissions later in iOS Settings.

Retention

We keep account, voice ID, and profile data while your account is active. Alarm audio may be regenerated and is not kept longer than needed to deliver the feature. Contact form messages are kept so we can help you and improve support quality.

Your choices and rights

  • Decline microphone, speech, or notification permissions.
  • Cancel a subscription in Apple ID → Subscriptions (Apple handles billing).
  • Delete your account in the App: Menu → Account → Delete account. That removes your server profile and voice clone where technically possible.
  • Request help, access, or deletion via the Contact Us form.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our processors may process data in the United States or other countries. By using the App you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations with appropriate safeguards used by those providers.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Privacy and support: Contact Us form.
