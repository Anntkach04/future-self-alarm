Privacy Policy

Future Self Alarm · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Future Self Alarm (“the App”) helps you wake up with a message spoken in a voice clone of your own voice. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Who we are

The App is operated by the Future Self Alarm team. For privacy questions, tap {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} — Mail opens with the subject already filled in.

What we collect

  • Account. If you Sign in with Apple, we store your Apple user identifier and, if Apple provides it, your email.
  • Voice sample. Audio you record to create a voice clone is sent to our servers and to our voice provider (ElevenLabs) to create and use your clone. We store the resulting voice ID so we can generate morning alarms.
  • Profile answers. Name, goals, tone preferences, music bed choice, and similar onboarding answers used to personalize scripts.
  • Mood check-ins. Optional mood and note that can shape the next morning’s wake message.
  • Subscription status. Handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Device / diagnostics. Basic technical logs needed to run the service (for example API errors). We do not sell personal data.

How we use data

  • To clone your voice and generate alarm audio
  • To personalize morning scripts and mood-based lines
  • To restore your profile after you sign in again
  • To provide and manage membership features

Processors

We use trusted processors to run the App, including:

  • ElevenLabs (voice cloning and text-to-speech)
  • OpenAI (short mood encouragement text, when enabled)
  • Our hosting provider for the API and database
  • RevenueCat and Apple for subscriptions

Retention

We keep account, voice ID, and profile data while your account is active. You can request deletion by emailing us. Alarm audio may be regenerated and is not kept longer than needed to deliver the feature.

Your choices

  • You can decline microphone or notification permissions (features will be limited).
  • You can cancel a subscription in Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Menu → Account → Delete account. That removes your server profile and voice clone. Need help? Email us (subject is filled in automatically).

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
